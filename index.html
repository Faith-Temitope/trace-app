What it does

Pair face to faceScan each other's QR code. Only people you accept can see you.
Works with no dataYour phone keeps recording. With data it sends; without data it texts your last location by SMS.
Hidden SOSPress the power button 4 times, or tap a disguised tile. Your contacts are texted at once.
"Gone quiet" alarmIf your phone stops reporting, your contacts' phones notice and warn them, even if yours is off.
Journey check-inSay where you're going and when you'll arrive. Miss it and your people are alerted.
Built for dangerDuress PIN opens a fake empty app. Disguise the icon as a calculator. A SIM swap is reported from the new SIM.

How to install

  1. Tap Download above. If your phone asks, allow your browser (or WhatsApp / Files) to install unknown apps.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. Google Play Protect may say the developer isn't recognised. Tap More details → Install anyway. This appears for any app installed outside the Play Store.
  4. Open Trace and follow the setup. Choose "Allow all the time" for location, and set Trace to No restrictions in battery settings.

You can also share the app with anyone by Xender or WhatsApp. Send them the APK file.

Privacy

Locations are encrypted on your phone before they leave it. Only the people you've paired with can read them. The server can't. There's no email, no sign-up and no ads.

Normal SMS charges apply when Trace texts your contacts. You choose how often in Settings.
