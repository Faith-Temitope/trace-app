If something happens to you, your people will know where to look.

Trace is a free Android app. You pair it with family and friends you trust. Your phones quietly keep each other updated on where you are, including on roads with no network.

Why we made it

Too many families have had to ask the same question after someone was taken on the road: where was he last seen? Usually nobody knows. The phone was switched off, and nothing had been saved.

Trace saves it. Every few minutes it notes where your phone is and shares that with the people you've chosen. If your phone goes off, they still have the last place it was.

How it works

  1. Pair with someone in person. One of you shows a code on the screen and the other scans it. Nobody can follow you unless you've paired with them.
  2. Leave it running. Trace records your location every 10, 30 or 60 minutes, whichever you choose. You don't have to open it.
  3. If something goes wrong, your people see where you were, and Trace warns them, even when you can't.

When things go wrong

No data?

Your phone keeps recording. When there's no internet, Trace sends your last location to your people by normal SMS.

Phone switched off?

Your people's phones notice that you've stopped reporting and warn them, with the last place your phone was.

Can't open the app?

Press your power button four times quickly. Your phone buzzes once and your people get an SOS text with your location. Nothing shows on the screen.

Forced to unlock it?

Set a second PIN. If someone makes you open Trace, use that one. It opens an empty app and quietly sends SOS.

Travelling?

Tell Trace where you're going and when you should arrive. If you haven't checked in, your people are told, and they get your last location.

Want your family to watch your route?

Turn on Live route. Trace saves your location every minute and draws the road you take on their map, until you turn it off.

Someone changes your SIM?

Trace texts your people from the new SIM. They get your location and the new number.

Your mother uses a small phone?

Add her number in Trace. She gets your SOS and alerts by normal SMS, with a map link to where you were.

Want to know where someone is right now?

Tap "Ask where they are". Their phone sends a fresh location, even if Trace is closed on it.

What Trace can't do

Trace can't stop anyone from harming you, and it can't call the police for you. What it does is make sure the people who would come looking for you know where to start.

It needs your phone to have been on, with location turned on. On Tecno, Infinix and itel phones, you need to change one battery setting so the phone doesn't shut Trace down. The app shows you how during setup.

How to install

  1. Tap Download at the top of this page. If your phone asks, allow your browser (or WhatsApp, or Files) to install apps.
  2. Open the file you downloaded and tap Install.
  3. Your phone may warn that the app is from an unknown developer. That's because Trace isn't on the Play Store yet. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Trace and follow the setup. When it asks about location, choose Allow all the time.

Once you have it, you can send the app to anyone by Xender or WhatsApp.

Your privacy

Your location is locked on your phone before it's sent, and only the people you've paired with can unlock it. We can't read it. There's no sign-up, no email and no ads, and we don't sell anything about you.

What it costs

Trace is free. The only cost is the normal SMS charge from your network when Trace texts your people, and you choose how often that can happen.
